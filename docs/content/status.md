> `pck-reviewer` and Notes. Dates are recorded inside the relevant verdict cells
> rather than in a separate date column, and this table carries no link column.

> `.opencode/scratchpad/content/<area-id>/<kind>-status.md`. Scratchpad files are
> working notes only. Whenever a state, verdict, ID, flag, topic or coverage fact
> changes, the relevant canonical doc (`docs/content/status.md`,
> `docs/coverage/COVERAGE.md`, `docs/content/walkthrough.md` or
> `docs/content/content-bible.md`, as appropriate) must be updated too; a
> scratchpad file alone is not the record (content-rules.md W5).
